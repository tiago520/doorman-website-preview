A few good questions

Before you open the door.

What is the easiest way to get started?

Start with Doorman for Mac. The native preview brings browser sign-in, tool detection and diagnostics together. Supported connection automation is in development; the Mac page explains current availability and how to plan a team pilot.

Do I have to change my models?

No. Bring your provider keys and use the models your workflow needs. Routing can select a cheaper suitable model where confidence allows, and you can pin a model when you need to.

Can I see what is happening first?

Observe mode provides visibility without enabling routing changes. Coverage varies by client and deployment: tools that cannot be routed are identified as visibility-only.

What does Doorman cost?

Use your own provider keys and choose the plan your team needs. Your active subscription determines included usage and charges. See the full pricing and usage details.

Can we keep our existing AI router?

Yes, where its forwarding path is compatible. Keep provider access behind your existing router and evaluate Doorman for workforce coverage, governance and attribution. A shared downstream credential can lose individual identity; test the full path before promising per-person reporting. Compare deployment paths.

What does a useful first pilot prove?

Choose one team and representative work. Verify which tools are covered, who owns the recorded spend, what each policy does, and whether a proposed model change meets your quality criteria. Plan an AI visibility assessment.

Where can I read about security?

The security overview explains the controls and their boundaries, including retention and residency. SOC 2 remains in its observation period. Read the security overview.
