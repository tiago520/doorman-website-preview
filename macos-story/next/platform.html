Your questions

Start with a clear scope.

Can I keep my existing AI tools and providers?

For supported clients, use the gateway endpoint and approved credentials. Compatibility depends on the tool, protocol and model features. Start with the connection guide and verify one recorded request.

Who uses which part of Doorman?

Developers inspect requests and model fit; engineering managers set a pilot and budget; finance reviews attribution and close; security and IT review controls, identity and deployment coverage. Real workspace roles determine access.

Does adopting the gateway cover browser AI too?

Browser AI is a separate path. VibeGuard explains supported browser controls and its current availability. Installing a companion or discovering a domain does not prove that every interaction is governed.

What should a first pilot demonstrate?

A supported request reaches the correct workspace, its owner and cost are visible, the selected policies behave as expected, and the team knows how to recover the prior configuration. Expansion follows those checks.

How does Doorman fit an existing router?

Keep product inference on your current router while evaluating workforce coverage through supported endpoint and forwarding paths. A configured LiteLLM chain can add organization-level governance; shared upstream credentials do not establish per-person identity. For cooperative API-key-only CLI fleets, an ordinary router may already cover the connection need.
