Before you begin

Know what the report establishes.

Is AI Close a provider invoice?

No. It is a signed report of recorded AI activity with estimates and attributed savings. Reconcile it with provider invoices and the report's stated boundaries.

Does generating it change routing?

No. AI Close is a reporting action. It does not change the routing configuration or create a new model policy.

Can a signature prove that the period is complete?

No. A signature supports integrity verification. Completeness depends on recorded coverage, retention, row limits and the stated scope of the report.

What should finance check before sharing the report?

Confirm the workspace and UTC period, priced-model coverage, row limits, allocation and savings adjustments. Record unmeasured quality explicitly. The close guide covers generation, verification and the reconciliation handoff.
