One hierarchy. The homepage, product pages and console share a family, with scale matched to the task. Body copy stays regular; bold type signals a heading or action.

Product. Keep the same family at console density. Use tabular numerals for aligned values and the existing monospace stack for code. Do not substitute bold font files for regular text.

A consistent identity

Keep the shape. Keep the meaning.

One mark, wherever Doorman appears.

Preserve the geometry.

Use the supplied two-gate SVG. Keep its square corners, proportions and open center. Scale uniformly, with at least one stroke-width of clear space.

Choose the right treatment.

Use ink on light surfaces and white on dark ones. The three app containers belong to app icons. Keep the browser favicon neutral.

Make status truthful.

A state follows the application model, with a text label and shape cue. Animation does not prove protection. Reduced motion keeps every state readable.

Keep type familiar.

Use native San Francisco on Apple and genuine Inter elsewhere. The outlined wordmark masters keep the identity independent of installed fonts.
