Before you begin

Choose a rollout IT can verify.

Can Doorman route certificate-pinned apps?

A pinned app may be visible without its encrypted requests being available for routing. Use the reported coverage state and an actual request check.

Can we deploy the native Mac app today?

The native preview supports sign-in, status and diagnostics. Automatic connection setup and routing are still in development; a public installer and production MDM package are not available. Review the preview now and agree release requirements before fleet deployment.

Will Shadow AI discover every application?

No. Its findings depend on the traffic and endpoint signals visible to the configured deployment. Off-path activity and missing device reports remain coverage gaps.

What should IT agree before expansion?

Name the device groups, app owner, supported package, distribution method and recovery procedure. Check whether the verified request carries the identity needed for team budgets and cost allocation. Use the rollout guide to record the acceptance criteria.

Give every rollout a clear next step.

Bring one Mac group, its everyday AI tools and the IT owner. Plan an evaluation that connects a simple setup experience to verified coverage, useful reports and a decision on the next group.
