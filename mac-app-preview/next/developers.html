Before you start

A setup you can verify.

Check the fit for your tools, permissions and deployment.

Do I have to change my application code?

Many supported clients accept an endpoint or environment setting. Follow the client-specific guide; pinned applications and traffic outside the configured path are not automatically routed.

Can my agent ask about its budget?

The agent-facing MCP endpoint exposes scoped, read-only budget, route and task-cost information. Explore Doorman MCP ↗

Is the Mac app a required download?

The gateway connection and native companion are separate. Current Go + Swift build instructions are available; a signed public installer has not been verified. Check Mac availability ↗

How do I protect retrieved context?

Identify retrieved material explicitly, then test the retrieval policy’s monitor, sanitize or block outcome. Automatic recognition is heuristic and scanner failures preserve the original request. Configure retrieval protection ↗
