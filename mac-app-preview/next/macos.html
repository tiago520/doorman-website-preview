Your questions

An easy start. Clear expectations.

What happens after I install the app?

Open Doorman and sign in to the intended workspace. The app shows its connection state and guides the next supported setup step. Check coverage for each tool, then verify a request in your workspace. Available automation depends on the build and your organization’s policy.

Can IT install it for everyone?

MDM, company self-service and an IT-supplied installer are rollout paths to plan with us. Confirm the supported package, signing, permissions, configuration and update policy before assigning it to devices. A production MDM package is not published yet. Use the managed rollout checklist.

Do people need to change AI tools?

Keep using the tools your workflow needs. Doorman connects supported paths and shows the difference between governance and observation. Some tools require additional setup; installing the app does not make every connection governable.

Does the Mac app replace the dashboard?

The app handles the local connection and everyday controls. The dashboard brings together organization-wide requests, policies, budgets and reporting. You can open it directly from the menu bar.

Are these screenshots from a released app?

The gallery shows the approved interface design with example data. The SwiftUI implementation is under review with its real Go helper. Inspect the earlier native capture archive for the original source and capture provenance.
