Before you begin

Prepare evidence with a defined scope.

Does an evidence pack mean we are certified?

No. Evidence supports your assessment and an auditor's review. Doorman's own SOC 2 posture remains an observation period, not a completed certification claim.

How long is the audit log retained?

Retention is shown in the workspace and depends on the plan and configuration. Confirm the applicable period before using it as a compliance requirement.

Is every generated artifact complete?

Coverage depends on logged activity, selected period, retention and any stated truncation or data gap. Review those boundaries with the artifact.

Who can restore a configuration?

Restoration is an administrative action subject to workspace permissions. A reviewer can inspect evidence without assuming they have authority to change policy. Agree an owner and follow the configuration recovery checks.

Answer your next evidence request.

Bring the reviewer’s question, reporting period and required artifact. Agree what the records can establish before generating the pack.
