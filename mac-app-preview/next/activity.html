Before you begin

Make the record useful to the team.

Will every call be attributed to a task or PR?

No. Attribution depends on the metadata supplied by the connected tool or integration. Missing identifiers cannot be reconstructed reliably from the cost alone.

Does the website show my traffic?

No. Linked console views use labeled sample data. Connect your own tool and inspect a real request in your workspace to verify coverage.

What is a DCU?

A Doorman Compute Unit is a normalized task-cost measure shown alongside the available request-level evidence. It is distinct from your provider's token accounting.

How do I connect a task or pull request?

Supply the supported task and PR identifiers through your integration, then verify them on a recorded request and its rollup. Use the attribution guide; activity without an identifier should stay unattributed rather than being guessed.
