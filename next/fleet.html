Before you begin

The details that shape your rollout.

Can Doorman route certificate-pinned apps?

A pinned app may be visible without its encrypted requests being available for routing. Use the reported coverage state and an actual request check.

Does the macOS download install the whole fleet stack?

The native Go/Swift client is currently a source-evaluation workflow. Signed public distribution and helper capabilities must be confirmed before planning a managed rollout.

Will Shadow AI discover every application?

No. Its findings depend on the traffic and endpoint signals visible to the configured deployment. Off-path activity and missing device reports remain coverage gaps.
