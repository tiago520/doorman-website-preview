Pricing questions

Understand what is included.

Are provider model charges included?

No. Bring-your-own-key operation keeps provider inference billing with your provider. The calculator estimates the listed Teams platform and routed-usage components only.

Is the included allowance per seat?

No. The routed-request allowance is per organization per calendar month.

Are these contractual quotes?

No. These are current implementation list rates, captured on 5 September 2026. Your agreement and active billing configuration determine the final charges.

Which requests are billable?

The implemented usage meter counts billable routed work after the allowance; pure passthrough is not billed by that meter. Optional meters and storage have separate terms.
