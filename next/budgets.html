Before you connect

Know what the control covers.

Does a budget automatically block work?

No. Monitoring and warnings allow traffic to continue. Hard-block modes intentionally stop eligible requests when the cap applies.

What if several limits apply?

Applicable limits are evaluated together; the most restrictive limit wins.

Can a teammate raise their own cap?

Changing organization budgets requires the appropriate owner or administrator permission. Members can review their standing without receiving unrestricted editing rights.

Does this cover every provider charge?

No. It governs qualifying traffic through the gateway, not unrelated purchases or AI use outside that path.
