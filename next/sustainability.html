Before you start

An estimate with an explicit boundary.

Check the fit for your tools, permissions and deployment.

Is this measured electricity consumption?

No. It is an activity-based estimate using recorded tokens, model factors and regional assumptions; hardware, batching and load affect the real value.

Does this certify our ESG reporting?

No. Doorman provides estimated operational data and methodology for your review. Your reporting obligations, assurance and organizational boundary require separate assessment.

Can we claim a greener region automatically?

No. A regional counterfactual needs evidence for the original and served paths. Do not infer physical processing location from a marketing label.
