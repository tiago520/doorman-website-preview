Before you start

Count delivered savings, not proposed shortcuts.

Check the fit for your tools, permissions and deployment.

Does the batch option deliver async jobs today?

Current batch support records explicit eligibility and defines provider request shapes. The submit/poll/retrieve lifecycle is documented as deferred; this page does not promise a working managed batch service.

Do you count my existing cache discount?

It is recorded separately from Doorman-attributable savings. The savings breakdown also exposes qualifying clawbacks and tuned-inference surcharges.

Are all reports the same savings number?

No. The 60-day routing report and all-time multi-axis breakdown have different scopes. Compare the period and contribution labels before reconciling them to a finance report.

Where do subscription seats fit?

A subscription pool selects among eligible credentials; it is a different mechanism from token reduction. Supported provider lanes and account terms must be reviewed. Explore provider choices ↗
