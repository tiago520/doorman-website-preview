Trust questions

Claims with clear boundaries.

Is Doorman SOC 2 certified?

The documented status remains SOC 2 observation period. This website does not represent a completed certification or provide an audit report.

Can I review retention and residency?

Yes. Discuss the available settings and commitments for your intended deployment, and confirm the actual configuration before sending sensitive data.

What is public in this preview?

The website, illustrative planners, and sample-console tour. The preview does not contain your production data or provide administrative access to a real organization.

How do I start a security review?

Use the contact page to prepare an email with the controls and deployment requirements you need to discuss. No message is sent until you send it from your email client.
