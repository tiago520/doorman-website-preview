Before you start

Choose the delivery path deliberately.

Check the fit for your tools, permissions and deployment.

Is the model catalogue a fixed provider list?

No. Capabilities and rates change. Use the current model board and your provider agreement rather than a static marketing comparison.

Are Doorman Models rates publicly available here?

Commercial availability, supported regions and charges need confirmation. This site does not turn a proposed flat-rate model offer into a released plan.

Can Doorman use subscription seats?

The provider vault contains subscription-pool controls. Current documented runtime support is the Anthropic lane; other seat types and automated token refresh are not equivalent to shipped runtime support. Confirm provider terms and account eligibility.

Can we use a tuned model?

A separate governed tuning workflow records capabilities, pricing and evaluation evidence before eligible routing stages. Explore Tune ↗
