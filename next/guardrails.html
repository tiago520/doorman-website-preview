Three useful jobs

Make the next decision clearer.

01

See what the provider receives.

On supported paths, tokenization replaces configured detected values with request-scoped tokens and restores those values in the returned response. Inspect the round trip before enabling it.

02

Choose an explicit response.

Monitor a match, redact supported sensitive spans, or block a supported non-streaming response. Match the action to the risk and the traffic you actually handle.

03

Tune from evidence.

Review findings and false positives before broad enforcement. Test an example against the active rules, then use the audit record to understand what happened.

Before you connect

Know what the control covers.

Are streaming responses blocked or redacted?

These output controls enforce supported non-streaming responses. Streams are monitored and audited after delivery.

Does tokenization catch every secret?

No. It detects configured entity patterns on supported paths. Detection is not comprehensive, and a transform failure can pass the original request through.

Do regulatory presets certify compliance?

No. Presets select detector categories. Compliance depends on your deployment, processes and obligations, not a preset name.

Start with one real request.

Connect a tool, inspect its record, and decide what to bring under control next.
