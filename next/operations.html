Before you begin

The details that shape your rollout.

Does fallback guarantee zero downtime?

No. Availability depends on the gateway, network, configured providers and compatible alternatives. The request record shows the outcome that actually occurred.

Does the console update itself continuously?

The comparison console loads the current view and provides Refresh data for an explicit update. Animated website diagrams are illustrations, not telemetry.

Are agent quality scores always available?

They depend on sampled runs, judging configuration and evidence. Missing quality evidence is not a passing result.
