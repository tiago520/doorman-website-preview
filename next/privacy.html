Website privacy

Understand what this website handles.

This public preview is designed to explain the product without collecting your prompts, provider keys, or login credentials.

This website

What happens in your browser.

  1. 01

    Setup and boundary planners

    Inputs are processed locally. Downloaded worksheets are saved by your browser; the planners do not submit them to Doorman.

  2. 02

    Contact drafts

    The contact flow prepares an email link. Your email client opens the draft, and you decide whether to send it.

  3. 03

    Product tour

    The sample console uses demonstration data. Do not enter production secrets or private prompts into the public demo.

The hosting layer

Know what the page loads.

Who hosts this preview?

GitHub Pages serves the website assets. The current website serves its fonts locally. Historical comparison pages may load Google Fonts. Hosting services receive the connection information needed to deliver their resources.

Does the website set up tracking analytics?

This preview adds no analytics or advertising tracker. The sample console uses browser storage for its demo mode and interface preferences.

Is this the privacy policy for my production deployment?

No. This notice describes the public website preview. Data processing, retention, and privacy commitments for a real deployment are defined by its configuration and your applicable agreements.
