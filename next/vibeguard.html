Before you start

Validate fit before browser-wide rollout.

Check the fit for your tools, permissions and deployment.

Can I download it from a store here?

A browser extension and gateway integration exist in source. A verified public store release is not linked here; request a deployment review instead of installing an unverified package.

Does it cover every AI website?

No. It depends on specific supported platform integrations, installed permissions and page hooks. The manifest includes platforms such as Replit, Lovable, Bolt and v0, but a listed domain alone does not prove every workflow is covered.

Does all prompt content stay in the browser?

Do not assume that. Local detection is part of the design, but configurable message previews can be sent with metadata. Review the actual preview settings and collection policy before a pilot.

Is VibeGuard the same as Doorman Gateway?

No. VibeGuard acts inside supported browser pages. The gateway governs supported traffic forwarded through its connection path; the native Mac app is another separate client surface.
