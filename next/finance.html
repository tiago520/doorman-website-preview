Before you start

Numbers with a clear accounting boundary.

Check the fit for your tools, permissions and deployment.

Is an attributed saving a lower invoice?

It is a comparison with an estimated original-model baseline. Provider contracts, usage outside Doorman and applicable service fees can change the invoice. Read the methodology ↗

Can we include spend outside the gateway?

External-spend reporting accepts supported imports or connectors. Imported costs are additional accounting evidence; importing a bill does not mean those requests were governed. Explore external spend ↗

Does a signed close certify savings or quality?

No. It supports document verification. Unpriced models, truncated source data and unmeasured quality must remain visible in the report.

Can we report the carbon impact too?

Doorman Green estimates operational inference emissions from tokens, model and regional factors. It is an estimate with methodology and uncertainty. Explore Doorman Green ↗
