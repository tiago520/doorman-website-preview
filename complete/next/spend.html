Before you connect

Know what the control covers.

Are savings an invoice guarantee?

No. They compare recorded routed cost with an estimated original-model baseline. Your provider invoice, usage outside the gateway, and applicable charges can differ.

Do you count existing provider cache discounts?

Those are recorded separately and excluded from Doorman-attributable headline savings.

Is projected spend the final bill?

No. Forecasts extrapolate available usage history. Unexpected bursts and changing workloads can alter the result.

Can finance use this for allocation and monthly close?

Use department attribution and chargeback to connect recorded costs with their owner. Review external imports, commitments and adjustments, then generate AI Close. Reconcile the signed report with invoices; observed usage and imported accounting data have different coverage.
