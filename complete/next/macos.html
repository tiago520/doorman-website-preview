Your questions

A clear role for the native app.

What does the Mac app add to the console?

The menu bar keeps connection state, the latest receipt and relevant actions close to the work. Preferences covers local choices; the console provides the wider request, budget and organization record.

Does the Mac app run the gateway?

No. The current Go helper and Swift interface are clients of a separate gateway. A healthy app status does not replace a request check in the intended workspace. Read the native setup guide before evaluating a build.

What do Paused and Needs attention mean?

Paused presents the resume path reported by the helper. Needs attention points to a connection, proxy, certificate or helper issue that needs review. Use Check this Mac and its readable diagnostic result before changing settings.

Can my organization manage the setup?

Discuss endpoint ownership, permissions, gateway forwarding and available distribution with IT. The public page shows a native preview, not a released fleet installer. Cert-pinned or unsupported apps may remain observed only.
