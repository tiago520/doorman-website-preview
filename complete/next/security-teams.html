Before you start

Know exactly what is being enforced.

Check the fit for your tools, permissions and deployment.

What happens with streaming and hidden actions?

Output blocking/redaction and tool-authorization v1 enforcement apply to supported non-streaming paths. Streams are monitored or audited; direct local actions and provider-internal agent steps may be invisible.

Does a detector preset establish compliance?

No. Presets select detectors, and evidence packs document recorded activity. Neither is a certification or a substitute for reviewing your complete deployment and obligations.

What data does the browser extension send?

VibeGuard is a separate browser deployment with its own permissions and configurable message previews. Review it independently from gateway data controls. Understand VibeGuard ↗

What should our security review include?

Traffic coverage, credential storage, retention by data type, enabled controls, failure behavior and the specific evidence available to your role. Read the security overview ↗

Will an agent-payment policy intercept every purchase?

No. The advanced API governs declared intents submitted by an integrated agent, with opt-in policy, decisions and approval records. Doorman neither holds funds nor guarantees interception of purchases made outside that integration.
