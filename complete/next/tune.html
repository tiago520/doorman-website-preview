Before you start

Evaluate Tune before promising an outcome.

Check the fit for your tools, permissions and deployment.

Is fine-tuning ready in every account?

No. The older Tune → Model partner trainer still has a simulated integration. The separate governed tuned-model system has provider adapters and lifecycle gates; confirm target and deployment support before purchasing or submitting data.

Will Tune always reduce spend?

No. The workload, available evidence, provider prices and applicable fees determine whether a candidate helps. Projections and sampled judgments should inform an explicit deployment decision.

Does Tune require retained prompts?

Some training and export workflows need eligible recorded content. Data retention, consent and ZDR gates can prevent a dataset or evaluation; an empty or skipped result must not be presented as success.

What should I bring to a Tune evaluation?

Bring one task family, representative examples, the current baseline, acceptance criteria and your retention/residency requirements. An eligible corpus and a configured provider matter more than a broad “train on everything” objective.

How do we recover from a bad candidate?

Use the lifecycle’s rollback action for a governed tuned model, or the matching classifier rollback for a classifier change. Confirm subsequent requests use the expected model or classifier; the two workflows have different version records. Follow the evaluation guide ↗
