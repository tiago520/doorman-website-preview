Prepare a reviewable monthly AI close.

Allocate recorded costs, inspect the report’s qualifications, and preserve a verified period document for your finance review.

Agree on the period and sources.

Use a gateway account with organization-admin access to generate AI Close. Choose the UTC month you are reviewing, confirm the organization, and identify the provider invoices or external accounting inputs needed alongside gateway usage.

  • Resolve departments and unassigned spend before relying on chargeback.
  • Check that relevant requests and pricing are present for the period.
  • Keep usage outside the gateway as a separate source of accounting evidence.
  • Identify the owner who will review quality, coverage and missing-data notices.
Inspect sample department chargeback ↗

Generate the period document.

  1. Open Spend → AI Close in your gateway console.
  2. Select the UTC month and choose Generate.
  3. Wait for the signed document, then inspect its period, spend, savings and inventory.
  4. Review the verification result and download the JSON when the document is ready.

Generation reads recorded activity; it does not change routing. The public sample console demonstrates the flow with example data and does not generate your organization’s actual report.

Try a sample monthly close ↗

Know what the request asks for.

The console uses POST /api/ai-close; an explicit-org form is also available at /api/orgs/:id/ai-close. Browser requests use the authenticated admin session and matching CSRF token.

{
  "period": "2026-08",
  "sample": false
}

This example selects August 2026 in UTC. Replace the period with the month you intend to close. sample: false requests no new quality sampling; existing evaluation evidence can still be included according to its calibration and eligibility. A deployment may expose additional sampling controls that require separate usage approval.

Read the qualifications beside the numbers.

Field or noticeQuestion to resolve
Period and recorded spendAre these the intended UTC boundaries and organization?
Honest savings and adjustmentsWhich original-model baseline, contributions, clawbacks and stated fees are included?
Unpriced requests or modelsWhich amounts cannot be priced reliably, and how material is the gap?
Truncated source recordsDid the report reach its source-row cap? Retain the completeness notice.
Quality evidenceWhich criteria were calibrated and eligible; which results are unmeasured, skipped or incomplete?
Inventory and coverageWhich models/tools came from traffic Doorman actually proxied in this window?

Do not replace unknown quality with a passing score or treat an all-time savings headline as the selected month’s number. The exact report fields and their adjustments govern the interpretation.

Verify the unchanged document.

The verifier uses POST /api/public/ai-close/verify with the downloaded object under the close field. The console requests verification after generation. Preserve the original JSON rather than editing values before sharing it.

A valid HMAC signature supports the document’s integrity under the gateway’s verification mechanism. It does not certify the completeness of all organizational AI usage, the accuracy of a provider invoice, savings achieved outside the gateway or independently assured quality.

Resolve gaps before sign-off.

If you seeNext action
Access deniedConfirm the organization and administrator role; do not reuse another team’s report.
An empty periodCheck UTC boundaries and recorded traffic. A valid empty report is not proof of zero usage outside the gateway.
A missing price or truncated reportRetain the notice, investigate the source and obtain the additional evidence your review needs.
Invalid signature or unavailable verifierKeep the original artifact, retry against the correct deployment and investigate before sharing a “verified” claim.
A mismatch with the invoiceCompare scope, provider contracts, external usage, subscriptions, credits and the report’s fee treatment.
