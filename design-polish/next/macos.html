Understand your AI footprint

More than your coding assistant.

Coding, conversations and browser AI all matter. App discovery tells you what is present; coverage tells you what Doorman can actually control.

Coding tools and agents

Keep the tools that ship your work.

Claude Code, Codex and other compatible clients can use a governed gateway path. Supported setup recipes handle the connection; an app restart or additional permission may still be needed.

AI chats

Give everyday AI a place.

Desktop conversations belong in the inventory too. Some connections are visible only: certificate-pinned apps such as ChatGPT Desktop cannot be treated as routed, inspected or governed traffic.

Browser AI

Include the work in browser tabs.

Review services such as ChatGPT and Claude on the web alongside desktop tools. Coverage depends on the browser, connection path and permissions—not simply having Doorman installed.

Shadow AI

Find activity worth a closer look.

Use detected apps and AI activity on observed network paths to discover tools outside your approved list. An observed connection is a lead to investigate, not proof of prompt contents or exact spend.

Before you get started

A simple start, with clear expectations.

Do I need to rewrite applications or change models?

The development build manages supported shell settings and verifies Claude Code in a fresh default zsh terminal. Open a new terminal after configuration. Existing terminals, custom shells and other tools need separate checks; Codex and Gemini are not marked verified by this check. API services can also connect directly with a governed endpoint; see the API quickstart.

Does installing Doorman govern every app?

No. Detection, observation and governance are different. A governed request must use a supported gateway path; certificate-pinned or opaque connections may expose only limited metadata. Review the app coverage and verify a representative request before relying on a policy or report.

Can we use our existing network and device management?

Make those part of the pilot. Review compatibility with your VPN, secure web gateway and endpoint policies before enabling a new path. MDM and company self-service distribution need an agreed package, permissions and update plan. Use the managed deployment checklist.

Where do team budgets and policies live?

The Mac app handles the local connection and its checks. The shared console brings together requests, policies, budgets and reporting for gateway-covered traffic. Explore the console with sample data.
