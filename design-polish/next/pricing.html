Pricing questions

Understand what is included.

Are provider model charges included?

No. Bring-your-own-key operation keeps provider inference billing with your provider. The calculator estimates the listed Teams platform and routed-usage components only.

Is the included allowance per seat?

No. The allowance is shared by the organization for each calendar month. It applies when your Doorman subscription is active and its billing setup supports the allowance. Have your workspace administrator confirm the active plan and included requests before use; creating an account alone does not activate them.

Are these contractual quotes?

No. These list rates reflect the billing implementation reviewed on 5 September 2026. Your agreement and active billing configuration determine the final charges.

How should I evaluate optional products?

Start with the capability you need, then confirm whether your plan includes it or charges separately. Review its usage limits and pricing before enabling it. Browse the product catalogue ↗ · See the finance workflow ↗

Which requests are billable?

The routed-usage meter counts successful requests where Doorman changes the model, injects cache instructions or evaluates a policy. The plan’s included allowance is deducted before the applicable routed-request charge. Pure passthrough is not billed by this meter; optional products and storage have separate terms.
