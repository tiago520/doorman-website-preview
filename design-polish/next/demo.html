What the tour proves

Explore freely. Verify in your workspace.

The sample explains the interface; the real gateway supplies your organization’s evidence.

Will the demo change our settings or bill us?

No. The public console uses sample data and local demonstration behavior. It does not connect to your gateway, create a paid subscription or apply a policy to your organization.

Why can a screenshot look different from the demo?

Screenshots preserve a particular example state. The interactive demo starts with its own sample state and changes as you explore. Both use example data; neither shows your organization.

What should I try after the tour?

Choose the Mac preview path to evaluate sign-in and local checks, or connect a supported client to an existing gateway. Verify recorded requests in your own authenticated console. Verify a real connection ↗
